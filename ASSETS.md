# NOVA imagery

The user supplied black, white, and bone lightning hoodie/sweatpants sets, two gray monogram tracksuit editorials, and the embroidered NOVA monogram logo. The storefront imagery was generated from those references with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Original reference files were preserved.

Photography uses generated imagery and the user-supplied gray monogram set reference. The campaign is shared by Home and lookbook frame 01; the gray monogram editorial is shared by Home, About, and frame 02; the black statement editorial is shared by Home and frame 04. The exact delivered files and dimensions are listed in [IMAGE-ASSETS.json](IMAGE-ASSETS.json). All references point to local optimized WebP files. No unrelated stock imagery is used.

The heather gray Monogram Zip Hoodie and Monogram Sweatpants are included as individual catalog products and a third coordinated set with separate sizes. Their front studio photos were generated with the built-in image tool from the supplied gray tracksuit and embroidered logo. `images/lookbook/nova-monogram-set-gray-studio.webp` is the generated coordinated set hero used on Home and Shop; it matches the black and bone sets' 5:4 studio composition, seamless background and soft lighting while preserving the gray zip hoodie, curved seams, monogram and open pant hems. `images/lookbook/nova-monogram-set-gray-lifestyle.webp` is the exact user-supplied `ChatGPT Image Oct 2, 2026, 11_58_36 PM-1.png`, encoded as WebP without cropping, recoloring or content edits. It remains in both individual product galleries. Lifestyle gallery views are labeled as user-supplied references; front views are labeled as generated visuals.

All placeholder references and photography-pending captions have been replaced. The transparent cutout of the supplied complete embroidered logo appears in the header, footer, and favicon. The complete built-in generation prompt set and reference roles are in [IMAGE-PROMPTS.json](IMAGE-PROMPTS.json). Full-resolution generated PNG originals are preserved in the sibling `NOVA-imagery/` deliverable directory. Only optimized WebP copies are bundled with the app; encoding preserves dimensions and the logo alpha channel.

The black and bone statement products follow the supplied lightning-logo designs and gothic back graphics. The gray editorials follow the supplied monogram tracksuits. Crystal and Essential hoodie images are synthetic demo concepts derived from the pullover silhouette and embroidered monogram; no exact references were supplied for those two products. They need approved design references before real commerce use.

Generated product photos are AI visualizations. Final artwork scale, print/thread treatment, materials, fabric weight, measurements, and fit require production confirmation. Product galleries and About disclose imagery provenance. Prices remain fictional, and checkout creates no real orders. The storefront is published on GitHub Pages after user-approved repository delivery.
